I offer services in data analysis, synthesis, and interpretation.

Services

  • Statistical analysis and visualization of time series, geospatial and satellite data, experimental results, environmental models, and machine learning results.

  • Scientific report writing.

  • Statistics

    • Causality analysis

    • Geospatial
